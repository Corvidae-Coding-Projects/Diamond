**Status:** Complete, 27 October 2026. Evidence: [LP8 ledger](../verification/lp8/README.md).

## Contract

LP8 freezes one source/artifact candidate and qualifies the complete language
profile rather than relabeling historical W/G/L observations. Forge and the
non-Git live workspace must be driven through their real browser, HTTP,
PostgreSQL, worker, and native-consumer paths with independent persisted-state
readback and restart/reconnect checks.

The final campaign also replays retained compiler, transport, authority, budget,
and recovery failures; executes fresh deterministic valid and hostile programs;
checks Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit where those actual engines are available;
and records keyboard, reflow, and generated accessibility relationships. Missing
engine or assistive-technology coverage is a named environmental gap, never
silently treated as a pass.

## Exit gate

AC-1--AC-12 have current-candidate evidence, no confirmed in-scope finding is
unresolved, the ordinary examples teach the safe path, and the source-to-
guarantee ledger records exact hashes, callers, recovery paths, seeds, budgets,
and unexercised boundaries. Infrastructure/deployment acceptance remains outside
this language checkpoint.
